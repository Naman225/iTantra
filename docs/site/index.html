THE PROBLEM

Voice communications collapse when disaster strikes or spectrum is congested.

During extreme floods, earthquakes, cyclones, or in remote Himalayan border passes, cellular networks and internet infrastructure fail completely. Emergency responders, defense detachments, and stranded civilians are forced to fall back on low-bitrate radio links such as LoRa, HF, or tactical VHF.

Traditional digital voice requires at least 16,000 to 64,000 bits per second. Over low-power radio channels with throughput capped at 0.3 to 5.4 kbps, streaming raw audio is physically impossible, resulting in severe packet loss, distorted stutter, and complete radio silence.

01

Cellular & Cloud Dependency Fails: Cloud APIs like Whisper or Google Speech cannot operate with zero internet. Everything must execute locally on the phone's CPU.

02

Extreme LoRa Range Barrier: LoRa radios can transmit 10–15 km with milliwatt power, but only at ~1 kbps. Traditional voice codecs (Opus, AMR) cannot fit into this envelope.

03

Multilingual Barrier in Rescue: Rescue teams speaking Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, or Marathi need instant translation without manual radio operators relaying messages.

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

End-to-End Neural Radio Access Pipeline

From microphone acoustic capture to long-range RF broadcast and localized loudspeaker voice reconstruction.

STEP 1 & 2

On-Device Acoustic STT

Microphone audio (16 kHz 16-bit PCM) is analyzed directly by quantized on-device neural acoustic models (Vosk & Sherpa-ONNX). Runs 100% offline in under 120ms with 0.163 RTF (6.1× faster than real-time).

STEP 3 & 4

TantraPacket Framing & RadioBus

Recognized speech is packed into an 8-byte fixed binary envelope with 3-tier priority flags (Red SOS > Yellow Alert > Green Routine) and CRC-16 CCITT polynomial 0x1021, dispatched across LoRa, UDP, and Bluetooth.

STEP 5

On-Device Indic Voice Synthesis

The receiving radio node reconstructs natural spoken voice in the operator's chosen Indian regional language at 0.049 RTF (20.2× faster than real-time), with tactical continuous vibration for SOS alerts.

HOW IT WORKS

Seven tactical stages, from voice input to spoken voice output.

Audio is never streamed as raw waveform across the airwaves — each stage processes speech semantically in sub-second turnaround.

01
Microphone capture & VAD gate
audio/AudioRecordManager.kt
Microphone stream is ingested at 16 kHz 16-bit mono PCM. Energy VAD and Silero neural voice activity filters ambient background wind and engine noise.
02
Offline Acoustic STT Engine
engine/SpeechToTextManager.kt
Quantized on-device acoustic models transcribe spoken sentences locally in ~120 ms. Zero cloud API calls; zero network latency.
03
3-Tier Intelligent Priority Triage
emergency/KeywordClassifier.kt
Scans transcript for critical keywords ("khatra", "sos", "danger", "madad"). Automatically routes as Priority 1 Red SOS, Priority 2 Yellow Alert, or Routine Comms.
04
Binary Protocol Framing (TantraPacket)
protocol/TantraPacket.kt
Encapsulates text into a compact frame: Magic (0x54) + Flags + 16-bit Sequence Number + Payload Length + Payload + CRC-16 CCITT polynomial 0x1021.
05
Multi-Bearer Radio Mesh Dispatch
radio/RadioBus.kt · UdpRadioTransceiver.kt
Simultaneously transmits across Sub-GHz LoRa (10–15 km), Wi-Fi Hotspot UDP Broadcast (port 8888), and Bluetooth RFCOMM Serial SPP with 60-second duplicate suppression.
06
Cross-Language Semantic Translation
protocol/PhraseCodebook.kt
Maps operational disaster response phrases across 10 Indian regional languages, allowing a Hindi field operator to talk directly with a Tamil or Bengali team.
07
On-Device Indic Neural Voice Synthesis
engine/TextToSpeechManager.kt
High-fidelity offline neural voice engine reconstructs natural voice output at 0.049 RTF (20.2× faster than real-time) through the receiver phone's loudspeaker.

FIELD OCTETS TYPE FUNCTION & TACTICAL BEHAVIOR
Magic Byte 1 Byte uint8 Fixed delimiter 0x54 ('T' for Tantra) to immediately reject ambient RF static & garbage packets.
Flags 1 Byte bitfield Bit 7: Red SOS Distress | Bit 6: Yellow Tactical Alert | Bits 0–3: Indic Language Identifier (0–9).
Sequence Number 2 Bytes uint16 Monotonic counter (1..65534) for multi-path mesh echo deduplication and ordered delivery.
Payload Length 2 Bytes uint16 Byte count of compressed UTF-8 text tokens (typically 30–80 bytes).
Payload Tokens N Bytes bytes Compact UTF-8 text string representing the recognized spoken speech sentence.
CRC-16 CCITT 2 Bytes uint16 Mathematical polynomial check (0x1021). Corrupted RF frames are silently dropped.
TOTAL OVERHEAD 8 Bytes Header + CRC 99.92% bandwidth reduction compared to raw 64 kbps analog voice streams.

WHAT WE'RE HONEST ABOUT — AND CONTINUING TO OPTIMIZE

While iTantra achieves 99.92% bandwidth savings by sending microscopic semantic text frames (~50 bytes) instead of raw 64 kbps audio, speech-to-text accuracy naturally depends on ambient signal-to-noise ratio. In severe 10 dB acoustic noise (e.g. heavy cyclone gales or helicopter rotor wash), vocabulary recognition degrades by ~8–12% without dedicated directional mic hardware.

To overcome this in real deployments, iTantra includes a Dual-Layer Fallback Architecture: (1) An offline 100-phrase tactical operational codebook with 1-byte phrase IDs for critical commands, and (2) High-priority tactical continuous distress vibration that pulses aggressively without relying on audio or ringtones until acknowledged.
